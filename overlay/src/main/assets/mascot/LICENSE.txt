Bundled mascot character: the classic Shimeji

Frames: shime1.png ... shime46.png are from the original Shimeji desktop
mascot program by Yuki Yamada (http://www.group-finity.com/Shimeji).

This APK bundles a subset of those frames (see poses.json and
docs/character-plan.md in the source repository).
